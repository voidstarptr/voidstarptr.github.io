Methodology

Entry: all four conditionsVZO rises for N consecutive bars; MACD above its signal after a recent crossover (configurable); close above EMA and SMA by the selected buffer; close at least X% above the lowest low of the preceding N bars. Optional prior capitulation and weekly EMA gate.
Exit: selectable invalidationExit to cash when the selected trend or momentum condition fails. Stay long until exit; no daily churn from the entry conditions alone.
Execution and warm-upOrders fill at the next session's open, with fractional shares. Pre-range data establishes the initial regime; insufficient history is flagged. No final liquidation is forced. Cash yield is an effective annual rate, accrued between trading dates.
Data conventionsUse consistently split/dividend-adjusted OHLCV for total-return research. CSV adjustment scales OHLC with Adj Close but leaves supplied volume unchanged. Taxes, spreads beyond configured costs, and liquidity limits are excluded.
Definitions and limitations

VZO = 100 × EMA(signed volume, N) / EMA(volume, N), where signed volume is the current volume times the sign of the close change. MACD = EMA(fast) − EMA(slow), with an EMA signal line. MACD crossover may precede other conditions by the selected number of bars; zero permits any bullish regime. Weekly volume is aggregated from daily volume; a week is used for execution only once the next trading week begins. Data should contain every completed exchange session. The demo has artificial weekday bars and is not an exchange calendar or actual ticker history. No survivorship correction or parameter optimization is included. The exit rule, thresholds, and numerical interpretation of a rapid reversal are illustrative design decisions.
